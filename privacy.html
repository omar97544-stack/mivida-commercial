Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Maqtani Estates, an independent real estate brokerage based in New Cairo, Egypt. It is not operated by Emaar Misr, the developer of Mivida. You can contact us on +20 101 033 3215 (phone and WhatsApp).

What we collect

  • Details you submit in the enquiry form: your name, mobile number, optional email address, the unit you are interested in, your buyer profile and your preferred contact time.
  • Advertising and usage data: we use Google Ads tags, which set cookies and collect information such as the ad you clicked, your device and browser, and the actions you take on this page. These tags tell us whether our ads lead to enquiries.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the commercial units on this page and arrange viewings or consultations.
  • To measure and improve our advertising. When you submit the form, a hashed (scrambled) version of your phone number and email may be sent to Google to match your enquiry to an ad click ("Enhanced Conversions").

We do not sell your information. We do not use it to market unrelated products to you.

Who we share it with

  • Our property team handling your enquiry.
  • Service providers that run this website and store enquiries for us (Google, GitHub).
  • Where needed to progress a purchase you ask about, the property's owner or their legal representatives, and only with your agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, by calling or messaging +20 101 033 3215. You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
